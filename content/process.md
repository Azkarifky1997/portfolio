## Start with the decision.
icon: compass
What does the team need to decide, and what would change their mind? That shapes the objectives and the methods.

## Go to where people are.
icon: workshop
Interviews, observation and surveys, in person or through local researchers, in people's own language where I can.

## Find the pattern.
icon: systems
Affinity mapping, thematic analysis and survey analysis, with AI as a helper and every theme checked against the original data.

## Make it land.
icon: wireframe
Clear stories, journeys and recommendations a team can build, and a check afterwards that the insight survived into delivery.
