## What I always do

- **Go to where people are.** On the bus, in the village, at the counter. The best insights rarely come from a desk.
- **Design the whole service.** A screen is only the visible part. I map the people, processes, systems and data behind it.
- **Design for everyone.** I look for who a service might leave out, and design for very different levels of confidence, connectivity and digital skill.
- **Make it simple, and make it real.** Ideas only matter if they get delivered, so I design for what a team can actually build and run.
