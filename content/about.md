I'm a service designer and researcher with over five years of experience designing services that work for the people who use them and the teams who deliver them.

I map services end to end, from the customer's journey to the colleague processes, systems and data behind it. Inclusive design is the core of my practice: I use it to spot who a service might leave out, and to design for people with very different levels of confidence, connectivity and digital skill.

## Sri Lanka

I started my career in Sri Lanka. As Team Lead, Business Design at Innovation Quotient, I led and mentored a team of business designers on research, product and service design projects in fintech, insurance, mobile technology and agriculture.

I researched how rural communities used financial and insurance services, and what built or broke their trust. I co-led the design of Rural Loan for vanilla farmers in Papua New Guinea, and designed a repayment journey linking smallholder tea farmers with their tea factory.

## The UK

I completed an MA in Design Innovation at the University of South Wales with a Distinction, specialising in inclusive design. For my dissertation I partnered with the UX team at Principality Building Society in Cardiff, running workshops to build more inclusive UX practices.

Since 2023 I have worked at a research-led design and digital agency, where I am now Lead CX Research Consultant. I have redesigned member services for two financial professional membership bodies, mapping the operations behind every screen, and run test-and-learn studies with rural bus passengers for a county council.

## What I do

- Service design: end-to-end journeys, service blueprints, process mapping and business rules
- Research: interviews, field research, surveys, synthesis and test-and-learn studies
- Product design: user flows, wireframes, prototypes and functional specifications
- Inclusive and accessible design
