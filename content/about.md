# Hello, it's lovely to meet you!

I'm Azka, a CX research and service designer based in the UK. I'm curious by nature, I love a good plan, and my favourite thing is helping people understand people, whether they're vanilla farmers getting their first loan or ultra-wealthy property buyers in Saudi Arabia.

## Finance by day, jewellery by night

I have always been a creative person in a very analytical world. I began my career in finance, with a First Class degree in Applied Accounting, but I felt it held back my imagination. So I kept my creative side alive outside work, with short courses in fashion and interior design and a small jewellery design business I ran from home.

Then I joined a start-up in Sri Lanka as a business designer and discovered design thinking. It brought everything together: the business mind from finance and the creativity I had always had, all in service of people. My first field research project, with smallholder farmers who didn't even own smartphones, taught me the lesson I still design by: start with people, not technology.

## From tea estates to vanilla farms

I grew into the role of Team Lead, Business Design at Innovation Quotient, leading a team of business designers on projects in fintech, insurance, mobile technology and agriculture. Highlights included two long-term agritech projects with GSMA, co-leading the design of Rural Loan, a first loan for vanilla farmers in Papua New Guinea, and designing a repayment journey linking smallholder tea farmers with their tea factory.

## A new country, the same curiosity

I came to the UK to study an MA in Design Innovation at the University of South Wales, graduating with a Distinction and specialising in inclusive design. For my dissertation I partnered with the UX team at Principality Building Society in Cardiff, running workshops to build more inclusive UX practices.

Since 2023 I have worked at a research-led design and digital agency, growing from Product Marketing Consultant to CX Research Consultant and now Lead CX Research Consultant. I work across research and service design for clients in financial services, local government, transport, tourism, property and the public sector.

### Some UK projects I've loved

- **Membership journeys for professional bodies** Redesigned how members of two financial professional bodies join, qualify, upgrade and renew, mapping every process, data flow and team behind the website.
- **Need states for a global membership body** Ran longitudinal research across four countries to understand what new and long-standing members need, and turned it into a need-state tool the client's teams use to plan.
- **Getting more people on rural buses** Rode rural bus routes to talk to passengers, then ran three test-and-learn campaigns for a county council. Passenger numbers rose on every route, by up to around 40%.
- **Ultra-luxury property in Saudi Arabia** Led a mixed-methods study of ultra-wealthy property buyers, building behavioural personas and presenting the findings to the client's executive team. The work led to a framework agreement.
- **How people feel about AI** Ran a study of around 3,000 people on attitudes to AI, and designed a tool to measure how ready people are to adopt it.
- **Wellbeing economy in Wales** Researched what wellbeing means to people and communities across Wales, with around 700 respondents.
- **Understanding a city's visitors** Ran a brand perception study with over 1,000 respondents for a major UK city destination, and built a customer intelligence portal so the client's teams could explore insight by segment, need and journey.
- **Designing AI assistants** Designed AI assistants, chatbots and AI-driven search for client websites, helping people find what they need while keeping clear routes to human help.

The people could hardly be more different, from vanilla farmers to ultra-wealthy buyers. But the job is always the same: listen with empathy, find what really drives people's decisions, and turn it into something an organisation can act on.

I also lead my agency's internal sessions on using AI well in research and design: to work faster, while keeping the thinking and judgement human.

## What I always do

1. **Go to where people are.** On the bus, in the village, at the counter. The best insights rarely come from a desk.
2. **Design the whole service.** A screen is only the visible part. I map the people, processes, systems and data behind it.
3. **Design for everyone.** I look for who a service might leave out, and design for very different levels of confidence, connectivity and digital skill.
4. **Make it simple, and make it real.** Ideas only matter if they get delivered, so I design for what a team can actually build and run.
