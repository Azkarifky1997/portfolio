I'm a service designer and researcher with over five years of experience, first in Sri Lanka and now in the UK. I map services end to end, from the customer's journey to the internal processes, systems and data behind it.

Inclusive design is the core of my practice. I use it to spot who a service might leave out, and to design for people with very different levels of confidence, connectivity and digital skill. I hold an MA in Design Innovation (Distinction) from the University of South Wales.
