I'm a UX researcher with over five years' experience, first in Sri Lanka and now in the UK. I plan and run research in the field and remotely, from interviews and workshops to surveys and usability testing, and turn it into insight that helps teams make confident product and service decisions.

Inclusive design is the core of my practice. I look for who a product or service might leave out, and design research for people with very different levels of confidence, connectivity and digital skill. Because I've also designed services end to end, I make sure research lands in something a team can actually build.
