About the project

A loan built around farmers, not paperwork

GSMA AgriTech brought together a lender, a vanilla buyer that traded with about 3,000 farmers and an agritech company. Their goal was a minimum viable credit product built around farmers’ real needs. Our job was to find out what those needs were and design a service that worked for farmers, the bank and its staff.

The challenge

Banks need an identity farmers didn’t have

Most farmers had no ID, bank account, financial history or collateral. Some had no birth certificate. Loan sharks were often the only option, and a trip to the bank could take hours.

Research question
How might we offer vanilla farmers with no ID, bank account or collateral a first loan that fits their harvest, their lives and the people they already trust?

Key insights

What we heard from farmers

₭

No one lives on vanilla alone

Every farmer relied on other income, such as cocoa.

7%

Credit was new

Only 7% had ever taken a formal loan. Interest and repayment terms were unfamiliar.

2%

Few smartphones

48% had a mobile phone, and only 2% a smartphone.

!

Carrying cash is dangerous

Farmers risked attack carrying vanilla or cash home.

6+

Longer loans needed

Farmers and agents wanted 6 months or more, not the 2 to 3 we assumed.

♀

Women often had no money of their own

Where they did, they usually hid it at home.

Reflection

What I’d do differently

Test the rules, not just the screens

Terms and fees decide whether someone can repay. Test them with users before launch.

Design for who’s missing

Act on what we heard about women from the start.

Make insights survive delivery

Agree up front how key findings will be checked in the live product.
